- **2026-10-05 · Dialect audit (step 3).** Under `-std=ibm`, 107/558 programs change stdout, but 105 of those are solely the `pretty-display` DISPLAY-formatting setting; only 2 are semantic (`nn12` intermediate precision via `arithmetic-osvs`; `ee04` binary truncation). Neither GnuCOBOL mode reproduces IBM's sign overpunch; true calibration needs IBM COBOL (owner procurement). Recommendation adopted: do **not** switch the oracle; run both dialects and flag the dialect-sensitive set.
- **2026-10-05 · Harness defects found while documenting the suite (steps 4/8).** (1) The 11 `.cbl.txt` negative probes are never executed by the sweep (it walks `.cbl` only). (2) `runCobol` accepts `opts.stdin` but never feeds it. (3) Missing toolchain → skip, not fail. All three → step 7.

Scala `BigDecimal` is DECIMAL128 (34 digits) vs Java exact — must be an explicit IR parameter. Whole corpus converts in ~1 s, so a golden snapshot of generated Scala is a cheap per-commit gate. Cost of option (a) ≈ 180 engineer-days (150–200). Designer's lean: (a), explicitly provisional on step-4 answers. **Not started** (owner decision); only the zero-output-change groundwork (golden snapshot) is being folded into step 7.

Class E (all-integer COMPUTE overflowing 32-bit ints in cobc) likewise. Both are being excluded from the fuzzer grammar.
